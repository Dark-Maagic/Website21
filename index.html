01 / Perspectives
“A master musician, must also conduct.”

I have found that in order to begin to innovate in intelligence, it becomes necessary not just to modify the way that you approach a problem, but also to modify the tools that you employ in approaching the problem.

For me, the goal is to create a set of efficient methods for problem-solving, giving room for future innovations--not mounting a collection of solutions with no direction.

03 / About

Engineering tools for higher-order reasoning.

Based in Brookline, Massachusetts, Gabriel Forman focuses on the cross-section of deep learning representations, mathematical systems, and high-performance computation.

By designing algorithmic workflows that minimize computational friction, the aim is to synthesize intuition and rigorous engineering into instruments capable of solving complex problems at scale.
