01 / Philosophy
“Form is not an arbitrary choice, but the direct result of resistance through geometry.”

Dieste turned clay, mortar, and steel into self-supporting thin-shell structures. By relying on double-curvature surfaces rather than heavy reinforcement, his vaults span immense spaces using minimal material.

His work stands at the intersection of rigorous mathematical calculation and profound humanism—what he termed an “architecture of necessity”.
